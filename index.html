Scammers reuse the same accounts. We connect the reports.

Saif Scam Radar collects scam reports, pulls out the links, phone numbers and bank accounts, and links reports that share them. One shared bank account means one campaign, and one campaign means one block request.

Try it: check a message

How it works

  1. Collect. People send in suspicious messages. Thousands can arrive at once.
  2. Extract. Every report is scanned for links, phone numbers and bank accounts.
  3. Connect. Reports sharing an account or link are grouped into one campaign.
  4. Act. The tool outputs a live blocklist and a summary for banks: the account, the victim count, the sample messages.

Why parallel processing matters

Each report can be checked on its own, so the work splits across cores. Reports that share an account go to the same worker, so no campaign is cut in half. Move the slider to see the effect on 500,000 reports.
